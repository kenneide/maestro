Hi, we're Alex and Alan. This is Maestro: live key, beat and cues from the DJ to the musician playing beside them.

The problem

Playing by ear,
watching for a nod.

  • New keyevery time the DJ mixes
  • Tempo & dropsshift without warning
  • Cuesare a nod across a dark stage
DJs with a live sax, violin or trumpet are everywhere now. But the musician is guessing. Every mix changes the key and the tempo, and their only cue is a nod across a dark, loud stage.

Maestro is

The only tool that puts the DJ's set in the musician's own key, with cues straight from the booth.

Maestro is the only tool that puts the DJ's set in the musician's own key, transposed for their instrument, with cues sent straight from the booth.

How it works

From the booth to the stage
in a fraction of a beat.

  1. djay Pro

    Streams track, key, tempo and position over OSC

  3. Maestro Cue plugin
    Ready

    DJ taps Stop, Ready, Play. Audio untouched

  5. Musician's phone
    C♯minor

    Key in their instrument, beat, waveform, cue

djay Pro streams what's playing over OSC. Our plugin sits in djay, where the DJ taps Stop, Ready or Play. The musician's phone shows the key in their instrument, the beat, and the cue, in a fraction of a beat.

What it makes possible

New ways to perform together.

Fearless key changes

DJs can mix across keys mid-set and the musician follows, already transposed.

Cue-driven arrangements

Solos in the breakdown, hits on the drop, all called from the booth.

Any set, any player

Musicians can join an unfamiliar set and still play with confidence.

This opens up new performances: DJs can change key mid-set and the musician follows. Breakdown solos and drop hits are called from the booth. And a musician can join a set they've never heard.
